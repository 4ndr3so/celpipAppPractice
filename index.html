CELPIP Speaking Performance Standards (Self-Evaluation Checklist)

1. Content / Coherence

Did you follow the task formula? Did you include concrete details, clear organization, and logical transitions?

2. Vocabulary

Did you use varied, precise terminology, idioms, and natural collocations without repeating words?

3. Listenability

Was your speech easy to understand with clear pronunciation, proper rhythm, and minimal pauses/fillers?

4. Task Fulfillment

Did you address every aspect of the prompt and match the appropriate tone for the persona?
